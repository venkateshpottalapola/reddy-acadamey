GOOD TO KNOW

A few questions,
answered.

How does the enquiry process work?＋

Share your contact information and each student's requirements using the enquiry form. When the backend database is configured and available, the form saves your details for REDDY ACADEMY to review. The form reports an error rather than showing success if the save cannot be confirmed.

Can I include more than one student?＋

Yes. Include up to 10 students, each with separate academic details.

Can I see a fee estimate now?＋

Yes. An estimated monthly fee is shown automatically in the Review step, based on your class, tuition type, weekly frequency and session length. The estimate updates whenever you change a selection. Final fees are confirmed by REDDY ACADEMY after reviewing your enquiry.

Are all listed subjects currently available?＋

Subject selections are recorded as described by the parent. The academy will confirm supported subjects during follow-up.
