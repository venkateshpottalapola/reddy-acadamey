REDDY ACADEMY · PRIVACY

Privacy notice.

This notice describes the information collected through a REDDY ACADEMY home-tuition enquiry, why it is used, and how to request a correction or deletion. The enquiry service requires a configured backend and database; a submission is confirmed only after the server reports that it was saved.

Information you may provide

An enquiry may include a parent or guardian's name and phone number, optional email address and preferred contact method; each student's optional name, class, board, stream, subjects of interest and tuition type; tutor preferences; city, locality, PIN code and optional address; and an optional message. The service also records consent, its time, a privacy-notice version, the enquiry's reference and status, and the submission time. The system currently does not calculate estimated fees; no fee is collected by the form.

How the information is intended to be used

When the service is configured and available, the submitted enquiry and associated student details are sent to the REDDY ACADEMY FastAPI backend and stored in its PostgreSQL database for authorized administrators to review, contact the parent, and manage follow-up. Contact details are used to respond using the contact preference selected. Enquiries are assigned a reference and status. Internal administrator notes are kept separately from parent-provided information and are not included in the public enquiry flow. No tutor match or fee is confirmed by submitting the form.

Storage, sharing and security

Enquiry information is intended to be stored in the PostgreSQL database configured by REDDY ACADEMY. Access to saved parent and student records is restricted to authenticated administrators. The website also loads photography and typefaces from external providers; page requests may disclose basic connection information to those providers, but enquiry form values are not included in those asset requests. A live operator must configure the backend, database, HTTPS, backups, and an appropriate retention process before accepting real enquiries. No retention period is represented as established by this draft.

Correction or deletion requests

To request correction or deletion of information previously sent to REDDY ACADEMY, email reddyacademy@gmail.com or call 98859 45863. Include the enquiry reference or enough information to help identify the enquiry, but do not send passwords or other sensitive credentials. The academy should verify the request and handle it through its authorized enquiry-management process.

Questions

For questions about this notice or an enquiry, contact reddyacademy@gmail.com.
